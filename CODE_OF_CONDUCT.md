# Code of conduct

Pantarr follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good faith, criticise ideas and not people, and keep the tickets about Pantarr.
Harassment, insults and the publication of someone's personal data are not accepted.

Report a problem with someone's behaviour privately to the maintainers: open a
[private security report](https://github.com/trunckz/pantarr/security/advisories/new) with « Conduct » in the title.
The maintainers may edit or remove comments, and block people who do not respect these rules.
